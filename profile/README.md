## What is Lipec?

Lipec is a free and open source 2D game creation and discovery platform. Build your own games with a clean, modern editor, share them with the world, and play what others have made.

We're building it in the open, so everything from the editor to the platform is yours to use, read and improve.

## Can I be a part of this?

Yeah for sure!

We accept contributions from the general public, and currently have a low standard on what we deem is appropriate to include or not.
